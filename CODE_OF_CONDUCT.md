# Code of Conduct

`labcheck` exists to make research and ML handoffs less fragile. That only works if people can report problems, propose checks, and discuss noisy findings without being made to feel foolish.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and evidence-oriented.
- Assume good intent when someone reports a false positive or confusing result.
- Treat findings as reproducibility signals, not personal criticism.
- Keep discussions focused on the repo, fixture, command, or documented behavior.
- Respect privacy. Do not post private datasets, credentials, unpublished reviewer material, or proprietary code in public issues.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Publicly shaming maintainers, students, labs, or companies because a repo has findings.
- Posting secrets, private data, or private repository contents.
- Using issues or discussions for spam, scraping, or unrelated promotion.

## Reporting

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the maintainer or open a private conversation if available.

For security-sensitive reports, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of a public issue.

Reports will be handled with context and proportionality. The goal is to keep the project useful, safe, and welcoming for researchers, founders, maintainers, students, and contributors.
